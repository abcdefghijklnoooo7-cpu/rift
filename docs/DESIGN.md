# Rift interface and world direction

The lobby takes its visual direction from RIVALS. Matchmaking sits above the 3D scene, session objectives and leaders sit along the edges, and navigation anchors the bottom. The space stays visible through translucent panels. The Arsenal, ability selection, settings, and pause view reuse the same shared components.

- Deep blue-grey surfaces, compact bold Gotham, and text outlines maintain readability.
- Green indicates play or completion, yellow marks attention and the active weapon, purple identifies arsenal and abilities, blue marks information and stamina, and red signals damage.
- Use simple typography, fine rules, and selective accent lines. Keep the combat view open.
- All 20 weapons are available through the Arsenal. Loadout selection is acknowledged by the server. Only the selected preview and equipped weapon models are built.
- The field brief tracks training visits, loadout changes, and the first match win during the current server session. Session leaders use match wins.
- Interactive screens respect safe insets and scale to fit. The weapon catalogue scrolls.
- HUD includes numbered 100 HP, stamina, ammo, weapon slots, ability cooldowns, and directional damage feedback.

The lobby and training zone use large native parts and readable signs. Training has distance lines, respawning targets, a movement course, trampolines, and a hazard area. Competitive matches keep their existing maps and server validation. Verification uses one Studio client.
